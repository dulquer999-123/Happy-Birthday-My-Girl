A memory I still laugh about

The Idukki escape.

Remember that trip to Idukki from Kidangoor? Your parents called, and suddenly we had to run out of the house and drive to a completely unknown place just so we could convince them that we were actually going somewhere.

Everything happened so quickly , the rushed call, the panic, trying to make the story sound believable, and then finally getting away from it all. But the part I remember most is the relief afterwards. We just laughed. That kind of laugh where you both know how ridiculous the whole situation was, and somehow surviving it together makes it even funnier.

I think that's one of the things I love about us. Even when we're stressed, scared, or completely unprepared, somehow we end up with a memory we'll laugh about for years.

Maybe I just really love travelling…

Or maybe I just really love you.

There was the day I rented a whole car and drove all the way from Kochi to Ettumanoor and back, just to have that time alone with you , driving around, talking, and simply getting to be together.

And then there was the time I heard you had gone shopping in Kottayam with your grandma. I didn't even know if I'd get to see you. I still took a bus to Kottayam, just hoping I might catch a glimpse of you from afar.

Looking back, I've travelled so many places and gone so many ridiculous distances whenever I wanted to see you that I have to ask myself: do I actually love travelling, or do I just love you?

I think we both know the answer.
